The Laws of Wyrmwood

All adventurers who enter Wyrmwood must follow the laws of the realm.

Rules inside Wyrmwood

  • Griefing is not allowed AT ALL. this includes 'writing' on the dynamap, griefing the wilderness or wilderness bases. However, you can do anything you want to the Resource World as long as it doesn't break any other rules.
  • Stealing is not allowed! (if the chest is unprotected it is fair game, but do not steal from other town member's chests and/or corpses).
  • Shop owners have the full rights to their shops, including Blacklisting players! However, they must have a good reason for doing this and have spoken to the player before Blacklisting them!).
  • Mayors can request a town plot to be returned to their ownership once a player has been offline for 30 days or more.
  • When a Mayor has been offline for more than 30 days a new member can request to become Mayor.
  • When a nation leader has been offline for more than 30 days, each town of the nation can submit one person to be the new nation leader, Which will be decided by a vote on discord.
  • Raiding of ruined towns is allowed and encouraged as the town will be deleted after 7 days if the mayor does not come back online. (Ruined towns can be seen by a ☢️ symbol on Dynamap.
  • If a player has died within your town, the town can take ownership of the loot but, entrapment is frowned upon.
  • As Wither farms have to built outside of town (Usually in other dimensions), they have a special no Griefing/Stealing at any time policy (We can check), However any build in the resource world will be overwritten on reset.

Discord Server Rules

  • Be respectful towards other members.
  • No spamming and no outside server promotion is allowed.
  • Do not post any inappropriate content.
  • Do not engage in any for of discrimintaion or hate speech. We have a zero tolerance policy for any kind of Homophobia, Transphobia and Sexism. This is not an exclusive list; generally, don't be unkind.
  • Follow the instructions of the server staff. Any decisions made by Owner or Admins are final. Please do not ask another staff member if you have already been told no.
  • Any form of cheating or exploiting is not allowed.
  • Use English only.
  • Self-promotion for content/art etc is allowed in the appropriate discord channel.
  • Swearing/bad language is not allowed.
  • When entering a giveaway you must only enter on ONE account and cannot coerce people into entering it for you (i.e to give you the prize if they win)
  • No politics, please. No matter what we all think about thing it's best not to bring up volatile topics in what should be a chill game.
