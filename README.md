PVP world tips and information

When you're killed you'll drop everything EXCEPT your Armor and whatever you were holding in your main hand and offhand at the moment you died.
Loot chests that have already spawned can be located by using the /lootchest locate command.
The name of the loot chest indicates what key it is possible to get from the chest (at a 20% chance per chest) and the general quality of loot.
Cosmetics are disabled in this world.
Flying is disabled in this world. (including elytra and mounts).
Backpack usage is disabled in this world.
When you want to leave you can do /home or /spawn.
You can view the PVP worldmap at play.wyrmwood.net:8000 making sure to select the PVP world in the right hand menu.

how to get a town and / or nation channel on discord

Towns:
Your town must have at least 5 members.
Your mayor must have been active in the last 30 days.
If the above criteria are met please create a ticket including each member's IGN and their discord username, as well as the name of the town and what emoji you would like in the channel title. [Every town with a town channel gets 10 bonus town blocks!]

Nations
Your nation must have at least 3 active towns
If the above criteria are met please create a ticket including each member's IGN and their discord username(of each town), as well as the name of the nation and what emoji you would like in the channel title.
