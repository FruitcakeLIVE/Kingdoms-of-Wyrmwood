The Beginning

Long ago before the rise of the Kingdoms, Wyrmwood was governed by Ancient Dragons, whose power shaped and ravaged the land itself. The Ancient magic of these Dragons still flow beneath the forests, still roar over the mountains and still echo in the caverns that explorers venture forth towards today. Come forth with us and uncover new secrets of Wyrmwood!
